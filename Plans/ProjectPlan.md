# Michael Bienasz Capstone

## Overview

This capstone project will focus on analyzing data on international matches as well as ELO rankings.

## Questions

1. Are underdog teams preforming better over time?


2. Are matches more high scoring or low scoring across decades?

3. Which nations are underperforming or overperforming there ELO?

4. Are winning margins increasing or decreasing between games?

5. How do these metrics compare across continents/confederation (Europe, South America, Asia, Africa)

## Dataset

Datasets were both found on Kaggle, and were retrieved on June 23, 2026. The Primary dataset contain international soccer match history from 1872 to 2026. The Secondary data set will contain ELO ratings for every team.



### Primary

Thomas, M. (2023). International football results from 1872 to 2026 [Data set]. Kaggle. Retrieved June 23, 2026, from https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/martj42/international-football-results-from-1872-to-2017

## Secondary

Al-Nimri, S. (2025). International football elo ratings (1872-2025) [Data set]. Kaggle. Retrieved June 23, 2026, from https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/saifalnimri/international-football-elo-ratings
